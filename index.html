HOW TO PLAY

GOAL
SAFEThe girls are chasing AJ! Run the winding Thunder Bay course and reach his SAFE SPACE (the glowing couch) before any girl gets there.
♥Each girl who tags AJ steals a heart. Fill the CAUGHT meter and he goes LOVESTRUCK (game over). Short invulnerability after each tag.
MAP3 neighbourhoods: WESTFORT (easy, 4 hearts), FORT WILLIAM (3 hearts), PORT ARTHUR (icy winter, 3 hearts). Street signs show where you are.
ON THE COURSE
JUMPJump fences; steer around dumpsters and trash cans; slush slows you; ice is slippery.
SLIDESlide under scaffold pipes. Hug the inside of bends on the yellow SHORTCUT arrows.
CANEnergy drink: refills BOOST + quick burst. SCOOTER: big speed for 6s. FANCY SHOES: faster + higher jumps for 8s.
POSTop-left shows your place, hearts and boost; the top bar shows everyone's progress to SAFE SPACE.
CONTROLS
LLeft thumb joystick steers (pull back to brake). AJ runs on his own.
▶BOOST hold (uses stamina) • JUMP • SLIDE • MENU pause.
